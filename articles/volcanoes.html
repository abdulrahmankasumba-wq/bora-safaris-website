Gorilla Trekking

Volcanoes National Park
Walk in the Footsteps of Mountain Gorillas

August 2026 8 min read Musanze, NW Rwanda

Few wildlife experiences in Africa can compare with standing quietly in a misty mountain forest while a family of mountain gorillas moves through the vegetation just a few metres away.

A mother watches over her young. A youngster climbs through the branches. A huge silverback sits calmly nearby, observing everything around him.

This is the magic of Volcanoes National Park, Rwanda's most famous national park and one of the world's premier destinations for mountain-gorilla trekking.

Located in northwestern Rwanda near Musanze, the park forms part of the magnificent Virunga volcanic mountain range, a landscape of mist-covered peaks, bamboo forests and ancient volcanic slopes.

"Come for the gorillas. Stay for the mountains. Leave with a memory that will last a lifetime."

Meet Rwanda's Mountain Gorillas

The mountain gorilla is undoubtedly the star of Volcanoes National Park.

Gorilla trekking begins with a journey into the forest, following trails through bamboo and montane vegetation. The trek itself can be part of the adventure. The terrain is mountainous, and the time required to reach a gorilla family can vary.

Then comes the moment every trekker has been waiting for. You enter the forest clearing and see them. A silverback sits peacefully. A mother cares for her infant. Young gorillas play and explore.

For a few precious moments, you are allowed to observe these remarkable animals in their natural environment. It is not simply a wildlife sighting. It is an encounter.

Key Facts About Volcanoes National Park

  • Premier Mountain Gorilla trekking destination
  • 5 major Virunga volcanic peaks (up to 4,507m)
  • Endangered Golden Monkey tracking
  • Historic Dian Fossey Karisoke Research Center site
  • Area: 160 km²
  • Elevation: 2,400–4,507 m
  • Location: Musanze, Northwestern Rwanda

The Majestic Virunga Mountains

Volcanoes National Park is named for the volcanic mountains that dominate the landscape.

The park contains five major volcanoes:

  • Mount Karisimbi – Highest peak at 4,507 metres
  • Mount Bisoke – Famous for its stunning crater lake
  • Mount Sabinyo – Border peak linking Rwanda, Uganda & DRC
  • Mount Gahinga – Beautiful bamboo slopes
  • Mount Muhabura – Iconic cone shape

These dramatic peaks create one of Rwanda's most recognizable landscapes. For adventurous travelers, hiking these volcanic mountains provides another way to experience the park beyond gorilla trekking.

Golden Monkey Tracking

The gorillas aren't the only primates worth searching for.

Volcanoes National Park is also home to the endangered golden monkey, a striking primate known for its distinctive golden-orange coloration.

Golden-monkey tracking takes visitors into the forest in search of these energetic animals as they move through the vegetation. It is an excellent experience for travelers who want to experience Rwanda's primate diversity while exploring another part of the park.

Follow in the Footsteps of Dian Fossey

Volcanoes National Park is closely associated with the pioneering primatologist Dian Fossey, whose work helped transform the world's understanding of mountain gorillas.

Visitors can explore the area associated with her former research station at Karisoke and learn about the history of gorilla conservation. The experience adds an important human dimension to the gorilla story.

You begin to understand that today's gorilla trekking industry is connected to decades of scientific research, conservation and efforts to protect these extraordinary animals.

More Than Gorillas

A visit to Volcanoes National Park doesn't have to end with wildlife.

Travelers can explore Buhanga Eco-Park, visit the Musanze Caves, hike the volcanoes, enjoy birdwatching or experience local culture and communities around the park. This makes the Volcanoes region an excellent destination for travelers who want to combine wildlife with adventure and cultural experiences.

Why Visit Volcanoes National Park?

  • Experience life-changing Mountain Gorilla trekking in the Virungas
  • Track energetic and rare Golden Monkeys in bamboo forests
  • Summit dramatic volcanic peaks like Mount Bisoke and Mount Karisimbi
  • Pay tribute to Dian Fossey's gorilla conservation legacy
  • Explore Musanze caves, Buhanga Eco-Park, and vibrant local cultural villages

If seeing mountain gorillas in the wild is on your bucket list, Volcanoes National Park belongs at the top of your African travel plans. It offers much more than a gorilla encounter: dramatic volcanoes, golden monkeys, rainforest adventures, cultural experiences and a fascinating conservation history.
