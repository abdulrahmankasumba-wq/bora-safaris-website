Nyungwe National Park
A Journey Into the Wild Heart of the Forest

August 2026 8 min read Southwestern Rwanda

Far from the open savannahs of eastern Rwanda lies a completely different world.

Mist hangs between ancient trees. Leaves rustle high above the forest floor. A distant call echoes through the canopy. Then, suddenly, a troop of monkeys moves through the branches overhead.

Welcome to Nyungwe National Park, one of Rwanda's most extraordinary natural treasures and one of Africa's remarkable rainforest destinations.

Located in southwestern Rwanda near the borders with Burundi and the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Nyungwe protects an enormous mountainous tropical rainforest filled with wildlife, waterfalls, birds and some of Africa's most fascinating primates.

"In Nyungwe, the adventure isn't waiting on the horizon — it is moving, calling, and rustling all around you."

Enter a World of Primates

For many travelers, the greatest attraction of Nyungwe is its extraordinary primate diversity.

The park is home to chimpanzees and 13 recorded primate species, including L'Hoest's monkey, an Albertine Rift endemic.

You may also encounter colobus monkeys, blue monkeys, mangabeys and other forest species as you explore the trails.

Chimpanzee tracking is one of the most exciting experiences available in Nyungwe. Unlike a conventional game drive where you scan open grasslands for animals, chimpanzee tracking takes you deep into the forest. You follow sounds, tracks and the movements of the animals through a living maze of vegetation.

Then comes the moment when you finally hear them. The calls of chimpanzees echoing through the forest can be unforgettable.

Key Facts About Nyungwe National Park

  • 13 recorded primate species (including Chimpanzees)
  • 322+ bird species (Albertine Rift endemics)
  • Famous 70-metre suspended Canopy Walkway
  • Ancient mountain rainforest ecosystem
  • Size: 1,019 km²
  • Elevation: 1,600–2,950 m
  • Location: Southwestern Rwanda

Walk Above the Rainforest

One of Nyungwe's most spectacular attractions is the Nyungwe Canopy Walkway.

Suspended high above the forest floor, the walkway provides an extraordinary view over the treetops. Instead of looking up at the rainforest, you find yourself looking across it.

The experience offers a completely different perspective of Nyungwe and creates fantastic opportunities for photography. On a clear day, the surrounding mountainous landscape stretches into the distance, while birds and monkeys may move through the canopy below and around you.

For travelers who enjoy adventure, nature and breathtaking scenery, the canopy walkway is one of the highlights of a visit to Rwanda.

A Forest Made for Exploration

Nyungwe is not a park you simply drive through. Its true character is discovered on foot.

Numerous trails lead visitors through the forest, past streams, waterfalls and dense vegetation. The atmosphere is peaceful and mysterious, with sunlight filtering through layers of leaves.

Every walk becomes an opportunity to discover something new. A butterfly may cross the trail. A bird may call from somewhere above. Monkeys may suddenly appear in the canopy. The forest rewards patience.

A Birdwatcher's Dream

Nyungwe is also one of Rwanda's most important destinations for birdwatching, with around 322 bird species reported by Visit Rwanda's birdwatching information.

Its location within the Albertine Rift makes it particularly important for bird diversity and endemic species. For birdwatchers, walking through Nyungwe is an opportunity to search for species that cannot easily be found in open savannah environments.

Waterfalls and Mountain Landscapes

Nyungwe's beauty isn't limited to its wildlife.

The mountainous terrain creates streams and waterfalls hidden within the forest. Hiking through the park can therefore combine wildlife encounters with spectacular natural scenery.

The cool, misty environment is also a welcome contrast to Rwanda's warmer lowland landscapes.

One of Africa's Ancient Forests

Nyungwe is particularly significant because of its great age and biodiversity. It represents an ancient rainforest ecosystem that has survived through immense changes in the surrounding landscape.

Today, protecting this forest is important not only for Rwanda but for the wider Albertine Rift region. When you visit Nyungwe, you aren't simply visiting a tourist attraction. You are stepping into an ecosystem that has existed for generations and supports an extraordinary collection of life.

Why Visit Nyungwe National Park?

  • Track wild chimpanzees deep inside an ancient rainforest
  • Walk 70 metres above the forest floor on the famous Canopy Walkway
  • Encounter 13 primate species including Ruwenzori colobus & L'Hoest's monkeys
  • Discover over 320 bird species with numerous Albertine Rift endemics
  • Hike scenic mountain trails to hidden forest waterfalls

Nyungwe is ideal for travelers who want something beyond the traditional safari. If your dream adventure involves chimpanzees, monkeys, rainforest trails, birds, waterfalls and walking high above the treetops, Nyungwe offers an unforgettable experience.
