Lake Mburo – Uganda's Compact Safari Gem

September 2024 6 min read Central-Western Uganda

Looking for a wildlife adventure close to Kampala, where you can experience stunning scenery, abundant wildlife, and a peaceful safari away from the crowds? Look no further than Lake Mburo National Park — Uganda's smallest savanna park with a big heart. Though compact in size, Lake Mburo packs an incredible variety of ecosystems into its borders. About 20% of the park is wetlands, forming part of a 50 km-long wetland system with swamps and five shimmering lakes.

Lake Mburo may be small, but it promises big adventures, breathtaking scenery, and intimate encounters with Uganda's wildlife — just 3.5 hours from Kampala.

Burchell's zebras — found nowhere else in Uganda — are one of Lake Mburo's most iconic residents.

Unique Wildlife and Birdlife

Lake Mburo is the only park in Uganda with Plains Zebra and Impala. Other wildlife includes Elands, Buffaloes, Waterbucks, Reedbucks, Hippos, Crocodiles, and elusive Leopards and Hyenas. Bird enthusiasts will find over 315 species including Southern Ground Hornbills, Black-bellied Bustards, and Rueppell's Starlings — best viewed around Rwonyo's acacia woodlands.

Key Facts

  • Uganda's only zebras and impalas
  • 315+ bird species
  • 20% wetlands ecosystem
  • 5 lakes within the park
  • 228 km from Kampala
  • No elephants — denser woodlands
  • Walking safaris permitted
  • 20% fees support communities

The serene shores of Lake Mburo — a compact gem offering boat safaris, walking safaris, and horseback rides.

Conservation and Getting There

Lake Mburo is a conservation success story — Uganda Wildlife Authority efforts have restored wildlife populations and 20% of entrance fees support local schools, clinics, and community projects. By road it's just 228 km (3.5 hours) from Kampala via Nshara Gate or Sanga Gate. Charter flights are available to Mbarara Airstrip. Inside the park, enjoy game drives, guided walking safaris, and boat rides on the lakes.

Why Visit?

  • See Uganda's only zebras and impalas in their natural habitat
  • Enjoy 315+ bird species for birdwatching lovers
  • Experience unique landscapes of savanna, wetlands, and woodlands
  • Easy access — just 3.5 hours from Kampala
  • Support conservation that benefits local communities
