Mgahinga Gorilla NP – Uganda's Hidden Mountain Paradise

December 2024 7 min read Southwestern Uganda

High in the misty mountains of southwestern Uganda lies Mgahinga Gorilla National Park — Uganda's smallest national park at just 33.7 km², yet one of its most magical. Perched at elevations ranging from 2,227 to 4,127 metres, the park is named from the local word 'Gahinga' meaning piles of volcanic stones. First declared a game sanctuary in 1930 and gazetted as a national park in 1991, Mgahinga was created to protect the rare Mountain Gorilla and the unique ecosystems of the Virunga Mountains.

Mgahinga may be small, but its beauty, wildlife, and cultural richness make it one of Uganda's most unforgettable safari destinations.

The rare golden monkey — found only in the Virunga Mountains, including Mgahinga Gorilla National Park.

Gorilla Trekking and the Rare Golden Monkey

One of the most unforgettable experiences in Mgahinga is trekking through bamboo forests and volcanic slopes in search of the park's habituated transboundary gorilla family. These gentle giants roam freely across the borders of Uganda, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Beyond gorillas, Mgahinga is also famous for the Golden Monkey — found only in the Virunga region, known for its striking golden-orange fur and energetic behaviour as it leaps through bamboo forests.

Key Facts

  • Uganda's smallest national park
  • 33.7 km² total area
  • Elevation: 2,227–4,127 m
  • 76+ mammal species
  • 180+ bird species
  • Borders Rwanda and DRC
  • Gazetted: 1991
  • Part of Virunga Conservation Area

The Majestic Virunga Volcanoes

Three towering extinct volcanoes dominate the park: Mount Muhabura, Mount Gahinga, and Mount Sabyinyo. Their steep slopes are covered with diverse vegetation zones ranging from bamboo forest to afro-alpine vegetation. Hiking these volcanoes rewards visitors with spectacular panoramic views across Uganda, Rwanda, and Congo.

Why Visit?

  • Trek endangered mountain gorillas in the wild
  • Track the rare and playful golden monkeys
  • Hike spectacular volcanoes in the Virunga Mountains
  • Discover rich wildlife and birdlife in a mountain ecosystem
  • Experience the fascinating Batwa cultural trail
  • Enjoy views across Uganda, Rwanda, and Congo
