Murchison Falls National Park
Uganda's Greatest Safari Adventure

April 2025 9 min read Northern Uganda

If you're searching for a safari adventure filled with dramatic landscapes, incredible wildlife, and unforgettable natural wonders, then Murchison Falls National Park is the perfect destination. As Uganda's largest and oldest national park, it offers visitors a spectacular blend of savanna plains, riverine forests, and one of the most powerful waterfalls in Africa.

Originally gazetted as a game reserve in 1926 and officially declared a national park in 1952, this magnificent wilderness lies at the northern end of the Albertine Rift Valley. Here, the Bunyoro Escarpment descends into sweeping palm-dotted plains, creating one of the most breathtaking landscapes in East Africa.

The Legendary Murchison Falls

At the heart of the park lies the spectacular Murchison Falls, one of the most powerful waterfalls on the African continent. Here, the mighty Victoria Nile is squeezed through a narrow 8-metre gorge before plunging 45 metres into the swirling Devil's Cauldron below. The thunderous roar of the water, combined with a permanent rainbow mist, creates one of the most dramatic natural spectacles in Africa.

"The thunderous roar of the falls, combined with a permanent rainbow mist, creates one of the most dramatic natural spectacles anywhere in Africa."

Diverse Ecosystems and Abundant Wildlife

Murchison Falls National Park is one of Uganda's most wildlife-rich destinations, hosting 144 mammal species, 556 bird species, 51 reptile species, and 51 amphibian species. The northern section features expansive savanna landscapes dotted with Borassus palms and acacia woodland, while the southern sector contains dense woodland sheltering primates and forest wildlife.

Wildlife Highlights

African Elephants — Over 900 individuals roam the park. Giraffes — particularly common in the northern savanna. African Buffaloes — with populations exceeding 10,000. Uganda Kob — one of the park's signature antelopes, with tens of thousands across the plains. Nile Crocodiles and Hippos — often seen basking along the Nile near the Albert Delta.

A Paradise for Bird Lovers

With over 556 recorded bird species, Murchison Falls is a dream destination for birdwatchers. One of the most sought-after sightings is the rare Shoebill, often spotted in the wetlands of the Albert Delta. Other remarkable species include the White-crested Turaco, African Fish Eagle, Goliath Heron, and Saddle-billed Stork.

One of 900+ African elephants that roam Murchison Falls National Park — Uganda's largest national park.

A Park Rich in History

Murchison Falls has attracted famous explorers and adventurers including Winston Churchill, Theodore Roosevelt, and Ernest Hemingway — who survived two plane crashes during his visit. The park also gained international fame when the classic film The African Queen, starring Humphrey Bogart and Katharine Hepburn, was filmed along the Nile and Lake Albert in 1951.

Why Visit Murchison Falls National Park?

  • Witness one of Africa's most powerful waterfalls
  • Explore Uganda's largest and most diverse national park
  • Experience thrilling game drives and boat safaris along the Nile
  • See incredible wildlife including elephants, giraffes, and crocodiles
  • Discover over 556 bird species including the rare Shoebill
  • Walk in the footsteps of legendary explorers and adventurers
