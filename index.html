Bwindi Impenetrable National Park – Sanctuary of the Mountain Gorilla

Deep in the mist-veiled highlands of southwestern Uganda lies one of Africa’s most ancient ecosystems. Believed to be over 25,000 years old, Bwindi’s primeval rainforest shelters half of the world’s surviving mountain gorillas. Here is your essential guide to trekking etiquette, seasonal timing, and the emotional power of this UNESCO World Heritage encounter.
