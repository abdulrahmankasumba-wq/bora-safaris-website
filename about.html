About Bora East Africa Safaris

At Bora East Africa Safaris, we believe travel is more than visiting destinations—it's about creating unforgettable memories, experiencing authentic cultures, and discovering the beauty and spirit of East Africa.

Based in Kampala, Uganda, Bora East Africa Safaris brings together experienced tourism professionals with a passion for creating meaningful and enjoyable travel experiences. Our team has extensive experience exploring Uganda and East Africa, giving our travelers knowledgeable guidance and a genuine understanding of the places we visit.

We specialize in flexible, carefully planned journeys including wildlife safaris, gorilla trekking, hiking, cultural experiences, nature adventures, and more. Whether you're traveling alone, with family, friends, or as a group, we can tailor your experience to your interests, schedule, and budget.

Our goal is simple: to make exploring East Africa enjoyable, accessible, and worth remembering.

Our Story

Built on Experience. Driven by Adventure.

Bora East Africa Safaris was born from a simple idea: great safari experiences don't have to be complicated or out of reach.

Behind Bora East Africa Safaris is a team of experienced tourism professionals who have spent years exploring Uganda and East Africa, guiding travelers through wildlife encounters, breathtaking landscapes, cultural experiences, and unforgettable adventures.

After years of working in the tourism industry, we wanted to create something of our own—a safari company that combines professional experience with a personal approach to travel.

Bora East Africa Safaris is still growing, but our foundation is built on years of experience, a genuine love for nature and adventure, and a commitment to giving every traveler a journey they will remember.

Meet Our Founder

Kakimba Harunah

Founder & Safari Guide

15+ Years of Tourism Experience

With over 15 years in the tourism industry, Kakimba Harunah has spent much of his career exploring Uganda and guiding travelers across East Africa.

His experience has taken him across all regions of Uganda as well as Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania, giving him a deep appreciation for the landscapes, wildlife, cultures, and people that make the region unique.

For Kakimba, tourism is more than a profession. It's something he genuinely enjoys. He loves being in nature, experiencing the excitement of a safari, meeting new people, and helping travelers discover places and experiences they may never have imagined.

This passion is one of the foundations of Bora East Africa Safaris.

Kakimba in the Field

Why Bora East Africa Safaris?

After years of working in tourism, Kakimba wanted to build a safari company where his experience could be combined with his passion for creating enjoyable and accessible travel experiences.

The vision is simple: give travelers a great safari without making the experience unnecessarily complicated or expensive.

Travel Should Be Fun, Personal, and Memorable.

We want our travelers to do more than simply visit a destination. We want them to laugh, explore, try new things, meet people, experience nature, and return home with stories they can't wait to share.

“We don't just want our guests to see East Africa. We want them to leave with a story worth telling.”
