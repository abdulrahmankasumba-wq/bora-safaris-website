THE STORY BEHIND BORA

Built on Experience. Driven by Adventure.

Bora East Africa Safaris grew from a straightforward belief: extraordinary African safari experiences should be personal, authentic, and free from unnecessary complication or inflated costs.

At the heart of this vision is Kakimba Harunah. With over 15 years in the East African tourism industry, Kakimba began with a passion for education and nature before dedicating his career to leading expeditions across Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania.

Over more than a decade in the field, he came to understand that a truly memorable safari is never just about vehicle hours or checking animals off a list. It is about understanding the delicate rhythms of the savannah, listening to the elders in rural villages, feeling the spray of the Victoria Nile, and sharing hearty laughter around an evening campfire.

“We don't just want our guests to see East Africa through a vehicle window. We want them to feel its heartbeat and leave with a story worth telling.”
— Kakimba Harunah, Founder

After years of navigating national parks and managing private tours, Kakimba founded Bora East Africa Safaris to offer a warmer, more responsive kind of safari company. Today, Bora remains grounded in that field-first ethos: handcrafted routes, respectful wildlife stewardship, and travel that enriches both the visitor and the local host communities.

WHERE WE OPERATE

Rooted in Uganda. Connected to Rwanda.

East Africa’s greatest biodiversity lies along the Albertine Rift. We specialize where our roots run deepest, connecting Uganda’s expansive savannahs and rainforests with Rwanda’s majestic volcanic peaks.

UGANDA
The Pearl of Africa

Wild, Diverse & Unrivaled

Home to half the world's remaining mountain gorillas in Bwindi, classic savannah game drives across Queen Elizabeth and Murchison Falls, chimpanzee tracking in Kibale, and the thundering Source of the Nile. Uganda offers unparalleled safari diversity across 10 distinct national parks.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Travel With Purpose

We believe that the wild places we explore must be protected for generations to come. Responsible travel is not an add-on at Bora—it is woven into how we plan, guide, and operate every journey.

Community First

We partner with village homestays, employ local scouts and drivers, and ensure safari revenues directly support families living around national parks.

Wildlife & Habitat Respect

Strict adherence to official wildlife tracking distances, park speed limits, and leave-no-trace wilderness protocols that prioritize animal welfare.

Living Cultural Heritage

Facilitating authentic, respectful interactions with local communities like our Nkonya village partners—sharing culinary traditions, dance, and daily life.

“Tourism should create opportunities for the people who protect these wild lands.”
