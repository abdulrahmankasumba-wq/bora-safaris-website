01 • WHO WE ARE

About Bora East Africa Safaris

Authentic wildlife journeys across Uganda & Rwanda

At Bora East Africa Safaris, we believe travel is more than visiting destinations—it's about creating unforgettable memories, experiencing authentic cultures, and discovering the beauty and spirit of East Africa.

Based in Kampala, Uganda, Bora East Africa Safaris brings together experienced tourism professionals with a passion for creating meaningful and enjoyable travel experiences. Our team has extensive experience exploring Uganda and East Africa, giving our travelers knowledgeable guidance and a genuine understanding of the places we visit.

We specialize in flexible, carefully planned journeys including wildlife safaris, gorilla trekking, chimpanzee habituation, mountain hiking, cultural immersion, nature adventures, and tailored private expeditions. Whether you're traveling alone, with family, friends, or as a group, we tailor your experience around your interests, schedule, and budget.

Our goal is simple: to make exploring East Africa enjoyable, accessible, and worth remembering for a lifetime.

02 • OUR ORIGINS

Our Story

Built on Experience. Driven by Adventure.

Bora East Africa Safaris was born from a simple idea: great safari experiences don't have to be complicated, rigid, or out of reach.

Behind Bora East Africa Safaris is a team of experienced tourism professionals who have spent years exploring Uganda and East Africa, guiding travelers through wildlife encounters, breathtaking landscapes, cultural experiences, and unforgettable adventures.

After years of working across the tourism sector, we wanted to create something of our own—a safari company that pairs seasoned field professionalism with an authentic, friendly, and deeply personal approach to travel.

Bora East Africa Safaris continues to grow, but our foundation remains rooted in years of hands-on field experience, a genuine love for nature and wildlife conservation, and an unwavering commitment to giving every traveler a journey they will treasure forever.

03 • LEADERSHIP

Meet Our Founder

Founder & Safari Guide 15+ Years Field Experience

Kakimba Harunah

With over 15 years in the tourism industry, Kakimba Harunah has spent much of his career exploring Uganda and guiding travelers from across the globe throughout East Africa.

His experience spans all regions and national parks of Uganda as well as Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania, giving him a deep appreciation for the landscapes, wildlife, cultures, and communities that make this region extraordinarily special.

For Kakimba, tourism is far more than a profession—it is a lifelong calling. He thrives in the wilderness, loves the thrill of wildlife tracking, and takes genuine joy in connecting with guests and helping them experience places they never imagined visiting.

This enduring passion and field-first mentality form the bedrock upon which Bora East Africa Safaris is built.

Kakimba in the Field

Why Bora East Africa Safaris?

After years of working in tourism, Kakimba wanted to build a safari company where his experience could be combined with his passion for creating enjoyable and accessible travel experiences.

The vision is simple: give travelers a great safari without making the experience unnecessarily complicated or expensive.

Travel Should Be Fun, Personal, and Memorable.

We want our travelers to do more than simply visit a destination. We want them to laugh, explore, try new things, meet people, experience nature, and return home with stories they can't wait to share.

“We don't just want our guests to see East Africa. We want them to leave with a story worth telling.”

— Kakimba Harunah, Founder

06 • OUR ETHOS

Travel With Purpose

Our Commitment to Responsible Tourism

We believe the places we visit should be enjoyed, respected, and protected. That's why we are committed to promoting responsible tourism that benefits local communities, respects wildlife, and helps preserve the natural and cultural heritage of East Africa.

Supporting Local Communities

We believe tourism should create opportunities for the people and communities who call these destinations home, hiring locally and supporting community initiatives.

Respecting Nature & Wildlife

We adhere to strict conservation and park guidelines, encouraging low-impact wildlife encounters that honor animals in their natural habitats.

Celebrating Local Cultures

We want travelers to connect meaningfully with the people, culinary traditions, stories, and living heritage that make each community unique.
