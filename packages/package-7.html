Your Safari Itinerary

Detailed day-by-day schedule for your 5 Days / 4 Nights journey:

Day 1

Transfer from Kampala to Kibale Forest National Park

  • Drive to Kibale (about 5–6 hours) with lunch in Fort Portal.
  • Enjoy views of the Rwenzori Mountains and tea plantations.
  • Check in and relax.
Day 2

Chimpanzee Trekking & Transfer to Queen Elizabeth

  • Morning chimpanzee trek in Kibale Forest (to be confirmed).
  • Optional Bigodi Wetland walk.
  • Continue to Queen Elizabeth National Park and check in.
Day 3

Game Drive & Transfer to Bwindi

  • Morning game drive for lions, elephants, buffaloes and antelopes.
  • Drive on to the Buhoma area of Bwindi and check in.
  • Overnight: Lodge (TBC).
Day 4

Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

  • Ranger briefing, then trek into the ancient Bwindi forest in search of a mountain gorilla family.
  • Spend one hour with the gorillas, observing their behaviour from a respectful distance.
  • Return to the lodge and relax.
  • Overnight: Lodge (TBC).
Day 5

Transfer to Kampala

  • After breakfast, travel from Bwindi to Kampala through southwestern and central Uganda.
  • Drop-off at Entebbe International Airport or a hotel of your choice.
