Your Safari Itinerary

Detailed day-by-day schedule for your 11 Days / 10 Nights journey:

Day 1

Arrival at Entebbe

  • Meet your Bora East Africa Safaris guide at Entebbe International Airport and transfer to a hotel of your choice.
  • Relax and prepare for the safari ahead.
Day 2

Transfer to Kibale Forest National Park

  • After breakfast, drive west to Kibale Forest, one of Uganda’s most important primate habitats.
  • Check in at your lodge.
Day 3

Chimpanzee Trekking in Kibale

  • Trek with an experienced guide in search of chimpanzees.
  • Return to the lodge for lunch and relax.
Day 4

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

  • Early breakfast, then drive to Queen Elizabeth, home to elephants, buffaloes, antelopes and lions.
  • Check in at your lodge.
Day 5

Morning Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Cruise

  • Morning game drive across the savannah plains.
  • Lunch at the lodge.
  • Afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel with hippos, crocodiles, elephants, buffaloes and birdlife.
Day 6

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest National Park

  • Travel via the Ishasha sector with a game drive in search of tree-climbing lions.
  • Check in at your lodge.
Day 7

Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

  • Ranger briefing, then trek into the ancient Bwindi forest in search of a mountain gorilla family.
  • Spend one hour with the gorillas, observing their behaviour from a respectful distance.
  • Return to the lodge for lunch.
  • Relax in the evening and prepare for the next day’s journey.
Day 8

Transfer to Mgahinga Gorilla National Park

  • Drive through the volcanic highlands of southwestern Uganda.
  • Peaceful canoe trip on Lake Mutanda.
  • Check in at your lodge.
  • Batwa community experience.
Day 9

Golden Monkey Tracking in Mgahinga

  • Trek through bamboo and montane forest to find the endangered golden monkeys.
  • The trek takes about 5–6 hours depending on where the monkeys are.
  • Return to the lodge.
Day 10

Transfer from Mgahinga to Kampala

  • After breakfast, drive from Kisoro to Kampala through changing landscapes.
  • Overnight at a hotel in Kampala or Entebbe (or drop-off at the airport for a late flight).
Day 11

Departure

  • Transfer to Entebbe International Airport for your departure flight.
