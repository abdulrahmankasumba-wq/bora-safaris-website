Your Safari Itinerary

Detailed day-by-day schedule for your 6 Days / 5 Nights journey:

Day 1

Arrival & Transfer to Akagera

  • Meet your guide at Kigali International Airport.
  • Drive to Akagera National Park and check in.
Day 2

Game Drive & Boat Cruise

  • Morning game drive for elephants, giraffes, zebras and buffaloes.
  • Afternoon boat cruise on Lake Ihema for hippos, crocodiles and birds.
Day 3

Transfer to Volcanoes National Park

  • Drive to Musanze.
  • Check in and prepare for gorilla trekking.
Day 4

Gorilla Trekking

  • Briefing at park headquarters, then trek in Volcanoes National Park in search of a mountain gorilla family.
  • Spend one hour with the gorillas.
  • Relax at the lodge.
Day 5

Guardian Village & Gisenyi

  • Visit the guardian village for local culture and craft.
  • Drive to Gisenyi on the shores of Lake Kivu.
Day 6

Return to Kigali & Departure

  • Drive back to Kigali.
  • Transfer to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
