Your Safari Itinerary

Detailed day-by-day schedule for your 14 Days / 13 Nights journey:

Day 1

Arrival at Entebbe

  • Meet your guide at Entebbe International Airport and transfer to your hotel.
Day 2

Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park

  • Drive north to Uganda’s largest national park.
  • Check in at your lodge.
Day 3

Game Drive

  • Game drive for elephants, giraffes, buffaloes and lions.
  • Optional Nile boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls.
Day 4

Transfer to Kibale National Park

  • Drive south via Hoima and Fort Portal.
  • Check in near Kibale.
Day 5

Chimpanzee Tracking & Bigodi Swamp Walk

  • Morning chimpanzee trek in Kibale Forest.
  • Afternoon guided walk in Bigodi Swamp for birds and monkeys.
Day 6

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park

  • Scenic drive to Queen Elizabeth.
  • Check in and relax.
Day 7

Game Drive

  • Game drive across the savannah for lions, elephants and buffaloes.
  • Optional Kazinga Channel cruise.
Day 8

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

  • Drive through the Ishasha sector, looking out for tree-climbing lions.
  • Check in at your lodge.
Day 9

Gorilla Trekking

  • Ranger briefing, then trek into Bwindi’s ancient forest in search of a mountain gorilla family.
  • Spend one hour with the gorillas.
  • Relax at the lodge.
Day 10

Transfer to Volcanoes National Park (Rwanda)

  • Cross the border into Rwanda.
  • Drive to Musanze and check in.
Day 11

Golden Monkey Trekking

  • Trek through bamboo forest to find the golden monkeys, a playful endangered primate.
  • Afternoon at leisure.
Day 12

Transfer to Lake Kivu

  • Drive to Lake Kivu.
  • Relax by the lake.
Day 13

Transfer to Kigali

  • Drive to Kigali.
  • Overnight in Kigali.
Day 14

City Tour & Departure

  • Kigali city tour.
  • Transfer to Kigali International Airport for your departure flight.
