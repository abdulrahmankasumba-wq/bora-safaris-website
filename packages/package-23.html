Private 4×4 Cruiser Dedicated Professional Guide Park Fees & Permits Included Customizable & Flexible Dates

Your Safari Itinerary

Day‑by‑day schedule for this 14‑day adventure.

Day 1

Arrival at Entebbe

  • Meet guide at Entebbe International Airport and transfer to hotel.
  • Relax and prepare for the adventure.
Day 2

Transfer to Murchison Falls via Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

  • Scenic drive north, optional rhino tracking at Ziwa.
  • Check‑in at lodge, dinner and overnight.
Day 3

Game Drive & Nile Boat Cruise

  • Morning game drive for the Big 5.
  • Afternoon boat cruise on the Nile to view Murchison Falls.
Day 4

Transfer to Kibale Forest

  • Drive through Hoima and Fort Portal, check‑in at lodge.
Day 5

Chimpanzee Tracking & Bigodi Wetland Walk

  • Morning chimp tracking in Kibale.
  • Afternoon wetland walk at Bigodi.
Day 6

Transfer to Queen Elizabeth NP

  • Scenic drive, check‑in at lodge.
Day 7

Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Cruise

  • Morning game drive.
  • Afternoon boat cruise on Kazinga Channel.
Day 8

Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable

  • Travel via Isasha sector, check‑in.
Day 9

Mountain Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

  • Gorilla tracking with experienced rangers.
  • Afternoon optional village visit.
Day 10

Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi

  • Scenic drive, check‑in, boat cruise to islands.
Day 11

Lake Bunyonyi Relaxation & Canoeing

  • Leisure day, optional community visit.
Day 12

Transfer to Lake Mburo NP

  • Drive to Lake Mburo, check‑in.
Day 13

Game Drive & Boat Ride on Lake Mburo

  • Morning game drive, afternoon boat ride.
Day 14

Return to Kampala or Entebbe

  • Stop at the Equator, then onward to airport.
