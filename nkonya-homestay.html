Welcome to Nkonya Village

Nestled in the lush green landscapes of Uganda, Nkonya Village Homestay offers a unique opportunity to live like a local. From the moment you arrive, you'll be welcomed as part of our extended family. Wake up to the sounds of nature, enjoy organic meals fresh from the garden, and participate in centuries-old cultural traditions. Whether you seek adventure or tranquility, Nkonya is your sanctuary.

Immersive Experiences

Discover the heart of our culture through hands-on activities and meaningful connections.

Reconnect with the earth. Sleep under the stars in a peaceful, off-grid environment, surrounded by the soothing sounds of the African night.

Outdoor Bathrooms & Modern Toilets

Experience the simplicity of village life without compromising on basic comforts. Our eco-friendly, open-air bathrooms offer a refreshing connection to nature.

  • Eco-friendly

Traditional Outdoor Kitchen

Gather around the fire as we prepare meals using traditional wood-fired methods. The kitchen is the heart of the home, and you're invited to be part of it.

  • Wood-fired cooking
  • Authentic Ugandan meals
  • Guests can participate

Cook Traditional Ugandan Meals

Learn the secrets of Ugandan cuisine. Harvest ingredients from our gardens and prepare hearty, organic dishes from scratch.

  • Matooke & Groundnut sauce
  • Posho
  • Sweet potatoes

Traditional Herbal Medicine

Discover the healing power of nature. Learn about indigenous plants and their uses in traditional African medicine.

  • Indigenous healing
  • Herbs & natural remedies
  • Cultural learning experience

Guided Village Walks

Stroll through the vibrant village paths. Meet the friendly locals, visit the village school, and explore lush banana plantations.

  • Meet local residents
  • Visit schools
